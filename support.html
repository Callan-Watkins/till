Common questions

Is Till really free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads, no subscription and nothing to unlock.

How do I add spending with Siri?

Say “Hey Siri, tell Till what I spent,” then say it like a text, for example “$14 at Chipotle.” Siri reads it back and waits for your yes before saving. You can also say “Add an expense in Till” or “How much have I spent in Till.” Turn Siri on or off in Till under Settings, Siri.

I added the wrong thing with Siri.

Say “Undo my last Till entry” within an hour, or open Till and swipe the entry to delete it.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. If you turn on iCloud sync, it's also kept in your own private iCloud so your devices match. Till has no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I back up or move to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud sync, or go to More, Backup and save a backup to Files. You can turn on automatic weekly backups there too. On the new iPhone, restore the backup from the same screen.

Can I import from another app or a spreadsheet?

Yes. Go to More, Import. Till reads Moneta exports, CSV spreadsheets and bank statement files.

How do I export my data?

Go to More, Export to save a CSV, a PDF report or a full backup.

I forgot my PIN.

On the lock screen, tap “Forgot your PIN?” and use your iPhone passcode to get back in. You can then set a new PIN in Settings, App Lock.

How do I delete everything?

Go to More, Settings, Data and storage and tap Delete all data. It erases Till on this iPhone and in your iCloud, so back up first if you might want it back.
